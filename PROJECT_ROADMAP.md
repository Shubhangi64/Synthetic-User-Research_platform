# Project Roadmap

## Milestone 1 — Planning and Design
Define the problem, demonstration product, target audience, system architecture, and research workflow.

## Milestone 2 — Synthetic-User Foundation
Build the experiment workspace, persona data model, persona generation, and multi-turn conversation context.

## Milestone 3 — Research Simulation, Insights, and Reporting
Complete survey and interview modes, question and consistency checks, response evaluation, insight dashboard, report exports, and end-to-end verification.

## Future work
- Compare synthetic responses with human-participant results.
- Measure agreement between automated and human quality ratings.
- Add persistent experiment storage and richer diversity analysis.
